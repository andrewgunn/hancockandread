About Hancock & Read

Designers and makers of bespoke furniture for homes and businesses across South Yorkshire, Derbyshire and beyond.

The people who make your furniture are the people who fit it, and it has been that way since 1985.

Our history

  1. 1985

    Where it began

    Hancock & Read was founded to serve the commercial sector, handcrafting and fitting bars, reception desks and specialist shop interiors.

  2. Home

    Into people's homes

    We soon realised our potential to create beautiful fitted furniture for the home. As the demand for kitchens and bedrooms grew, so did our workshop.

  3. Studio

    Design under one roof

    Our Sheffield workshop now houses its own design studio, so the people drawing your furniture work alongside the people making it.

  4. Today

    Further afield

    The majority of our projects come from repeat customers or recommendation. Word of mouth now takes us to Manchester, Leicestershire and Nottinghamshire, as well as Dore, Whirlow, Hope Valley and across South Yorkshire and Derbyshire.

How we work

i.

Made here

Every piece is made in our own workshop on Rugby Street in Sheffield.

ii.

Fitted by the makers

The craftsmen who build your furniture are the ones who install it.

iii.

Made the traditional way

Traditional mortise and tenon construction, with a wide choice of woods, colours, door fronts, worktops and handles.
